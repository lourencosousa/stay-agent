# Find

Search wide, with no fixed limit. The aim is every independent place that could fit the brief, ranked, so the waves can go from best to good without searching again.

## Where to look

Use web search and the places' own websites. Work through these, in this order, until new searches stop turning up new places:

1. **Search in English and in the local language.** For example "guesthouse Faial", "casa de campo Faial", "alojamento local Faial", "turismo rural Faial". Add the kind of place and anything from "take into account": "small hotel Faial sea view".
2. **Small hotel and design guides.** Mr and Mrs Smith, Design Hotels, Small Luxury Hotels, Tablet, Kid and Coe, Plum Guide, Unique Homestays, The Nomad Collection, and the region's own boutique guides.
3. **Official and local tourism sites.** The regional tourism board, rural tourism associations, local holiday rental registers (in Portugal: Turismo de Portugal, Visit Azores, casas de campo associations).
4. **Travel and design press.** Articles like "where to stay in <place>" from magazines and serious blogs. Use them to find names, then go to each place's own site.
5. **Maps.** Search the map listings for lodging in the area and note names that are not already on the list.

Never use a booking platform to contact a place. Platforms are fine for finding names, but the email must come from the place's own website.

## What to keep

A place is kept only if all of these are true:

- **Independent.** Not part of a chain or a big rental company.
- **A public email on its own website,** or in its official listing on the tourism board. Note where it was found. A contact form only (no address) goes on a separate "form only" list the user can use by hand.
- **Plausibly open in the dates.** Check the site for a season (many places close in winter). If the site says closed, drop it. If it says nothing, keep it and note "season unknown".
- **Fits the brief** on the hard points: the people fit, the kind of place, inside the area, nothing the user ruled out.

Leave out: chains, resorts with hundreds of rooms, places that only book through a platform, places with no way to email them, and anything the brief rules out.

## Score

Give each kept place a score out of 10 against the brief:

- **Character (0 to 4).** A place with its own character, of the kind asked for. Judge from its own photos and words, not from adjectives on a booking site.
- **Fit (0 to 3).** What the user asked to take into account, and the location against the place they named.
- **Value (0 to 2).** Against the budget, from any public price. Unknown price scores 1.
- **Season (0 to 1).** 1 if clearly open, 0 if unknown.

Write one line on why it fits, in plain words: "Stone house on the cliff above Varadouro, two bedrooms, wood stove, 10 minutes to Horta by car."
